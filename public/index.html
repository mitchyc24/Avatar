Avatar Call

Video chat as an avatar

Your camera animates a cartoon version of you, right here on your device. Your friend sees the avatar and hears your voice. Your camera video is never sent anywhere.

  • Nothing to install. Your friend just opens the link.
  • Voice, messages and avatar movement go directly between your two devices (peer-to-peer).
  • Works in current browsers on phones, tablets and computers.

Calls need the Avatar Call server running on your computer (./avatar --share, see the README). Cast to a TV works right here.

Get ready

Your browser will ask for permission. The camera only animates your avatar; video stays on this device.

Your avatar

Use your own drawing

Upload an SVG avatar and your face will animate it. Your friend sees it too.

Make one from a photo with AI
  1. Click Copy AI prompt.
  2. Open ChatGPT (or another AI that can see images and write SVG), attach a clear, front-facing photo of yourself, and paste the prompt.
  3. Save the avatar.svg it gives you and upload it here, or copy its SVG code and use Paste SVG code.
Download template

Your photo goes to the AI service you choose, not to this app. If a feature doesn't move, the checker above says which part ids are missing. Ask the AI to add them.

Cast to your TV

Your face animates your avatar here; the TV shows it full screen and plays your voice. Your computer or phone talks to the TV directly over your home network.

Use headphones or keep your microphone away from the TV, or the TV's sound will echo back into the mic.

Casting needs Chrome on a computer or Android phone, on the same Wi-Fi as a Chromecast or Google TV.
