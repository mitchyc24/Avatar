Avatar Call

Video chat as an avatar

Your camera animates a cartoon version of you, right here on your device. Your friend sees the avatar and hears your voice. Your camera video is never sent anywhere.

  • Nothing to install. Your friend just opens the link.
  • Voice, messages and avatar movement go directly between your two devices (peer-to-peer).
  • Works in current browsers on phones, tablets and computers.

Get ready

Your browser will ask for permission. The camera only animates your avatar; video stays on this device.
